One job, start to finish

The paperwork keeps up with the truck

A job moves in one direction. Each step hands the next one everything it needs, so nothing gets retyped and nothing gets forgotten between the first visit and the final payment.

  1. Step 01

    The lead lands

    A form on your website becomes a job in here — not a note you'll lose in the truck.

  2. Step 02

    You size up the work

    Quote visit on your phone. Photos, measurements, access notes, and what the customer actually asked for.

  3. Step 03

    The quote writes itself

    Pull materials and labour from your own price list, or start from a job you've quoted before. Itemised, or one flat price with the detail kept private.

  4. Step 04

    They sign it online

    The customer opens a link, reads your terms and signs. You keep the signature, the timestamp and the address it came from.

  5. Step 05 · Money

    The deposit clears

    The moment they accept, they can pay the deposit by card. It goes to your Stripe account, not ours.

  6. Step 06

    The work gets scheduled

    The job lands on the calendar and on somebody's list, with the materials ready for the truck.

  7. Step 07 · Money

    The balance gets billed

    Mark the job done and the remaining balance is raised automatically, net of the deposit. Nobody forgets to send the last invoice.

  8. Step 08

    The warranty is on record

    Parts, dates and provider stored against the customer. They get the summary; you get a reminder before it lapses.

Connected Home Ledger

Your books fill themselves in

ConnectedWorkOS and Connected Home Ledger are two halves of one system. Take a deposit or a final payment on a job here, and it posts into your business books there as income — dated, categorised, and net of anything you refunded. No CSV export, no entering the same payment twice, no shoebox in March.

Getting paid

The money is yours before it's ours

Payments run through your own Stripe account, connected in a couple of clicks. Deposits and final payments land in your bank on your schedule. We never hold your money, and card details never touch our servers.

Quotes

Priced from your own list

Your materials, your labour rates, your markup. Save a job you sell often as a package and start the next one from it.

Margin

You see the profit first

Cost sits beside price on every line — materials, labour, subs — so you know the margin before you send it, not after the job.

Refunds

Handled, and recorded

Refund all or part of a payment from the job it belongs to. The customer gets a receipt and your books follow.

How payments work: you connect a Stripe account — yours if you have one, or you create it during setup, and you agree to Stripe's terms directly with them. Payouts go to your bank on your schedule. Stripe's card processing fees are charged by Stripe and are separate from your subscription. Until you connect an account you can still quote and schedule work, but you cannot take card payments.

Everyone else
