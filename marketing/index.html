One job, start to finish

The paperwork keeps up with the truck

A job moves in one direction. Each step hands the next one everything it needs, so nothing gets retyped and nothing gets forgotten between the walkthrough and the deposit cheque.

  1. Step 01

    The lead lands

    A form on your website becomes a lead in here — not an email you'll lose on a job site.

  2. Step 02

    You walk the house

    Site survey on your phone. Room by room, photos, existing gear, Wi-Fi coverage, what the customer actually asked for.

  3. Step 03

    The estimate writes itself

    Pull parts and labour from your own catalogue, or start from a package you've sold before. Itemised, or one flat price with the line items kept private.

  4. Step 04

    They sign it online

    The customer opens a link, reads your terms and signs. You keep the signature, the timestamp and the address it came from.

  5. Step 05 · Money

    The deposit clears

    The moment they accept, they can pay the deposit by card. It goes to your Stripe account, not ours.

  6. Step 06

    The install gets scheduled

    The job lands on the calendar and on somebody's list, with the material list ready for the van.

  7. Step 07 · Money

    The balance gets billed

    Mark the install done and the remaining balance is raised automatically, net of the deposit. Nobody forgets to send the last invoice.

  8. Step 08

    The warranty is on record

    Equipment, dates and provider stored against the customer. They get the summary; you get a reminder before it lapses.

Connected Home Ledger

Your books fill themselves in

ConnectedWorkOS and Connected Home Ledger are two halves of one system. Take a deposit or a final payment on a job here, and it posts into your business books there as income — dated, categorised, and net of anything you refunded. No CSV export, no entering the same payment twice, no shoebox in March.

Getting paid

The money is yours before it's ours

Payments run through your own Stripe account, connected in a couple of clicks. Deposits and final payments land in your bank on your schedule. We never hold your money, and card details never touch our servers.

Margin

You see the profit first

Cost sits beside price on every line — materials, labour, subs — so you know the margin before you send it, not after the job.

Refunds

Handled, and recorded
