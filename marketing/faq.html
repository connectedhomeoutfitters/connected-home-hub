Questions

Straight answers

The things people actually ask before signing up. If yours is not here, ask it — the reply comes from a person.

Do I need a Stripe account?

To take card payments, yes. You connect one you already have, or create one during setup — it is free to open. Everything else (quoting, scheduling, invoicing, warranties) works without it, but the pay page will tell your customer that payment is unavailable until an account is connected.

What does it cost?

A flat subscription, billed as part of your Connected Home Ledger plan. We do not take a percentage of your invoices. Stripe charges its own per-transaction fee on card payments, deducted before payout — that is billed by Stripe, separately from us.

Do my customers need an account?

No. They get a link. They open the quote, sign it, pay the deposit and follow the job from that same link — no password, no app, nothing to sign up for.

Do I have to use Connected Home Ledger?

Today, yes — Ledger is where you sign up, and it is where your books live. ConnectedWorkOS opens from inside your Business Workspace, already signed in. That coupling is the point: payments you collect on a job post straight into your books instead of being re-keyed at tax time.

Where does my money go?

Into your own Stripe account, then to your bank on Stripe’s payout schedule. We never hold your funds. The connection lets us create charges on your behalf; it does not give us your money or your keys.

What trades is it for?

Any small service business that quotes work, schedules it and invoices for it — lawn care and landscaping, repair and handyman, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, pest control, appliance repair, pool and pressure washing. Nothing in the product assumes a particular trade: your price list, your job types, your terms.

Can my crew or subcontractors use it?

Yes. Staff get their own logins. Subcontractors get a separate portal that shows only the jobs assigned to them and their own paperwork — never your customer list or your pricing.

Can I hide my prices on a quote?

Per line, or for the whole job. A hidden line shows the customer as “Included” while still counting toward the total, and a flat-rate package shows one price with a bulleted list of what is in it. Your internal costs and margin stay visible to you either way.

What happens if I stop subscribing?

Access is suspended, not deleted. Your data stays where it is, so if you come back it is waiting for you.

Is my customer data used for anything else?

No. Each business is isolated from every other business on the platform, and customer documents are never indexed by search engines — the application sends a no-index instruction on every page and blocks crawlers outright.
